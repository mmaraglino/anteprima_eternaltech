TRUST BY DESIGN

Trust is a requirement,
not a promise.

When technology works on identity, voice, likeness and memory, trust enters the evaluation before the product does. So we treat it as a design principle — with the European regulatory framework as reference — and make it readable in five points.

Authorization

Who authorizes the use of content, likeness, voice and materials.

Sources and limits

Which sources feed the system, and which boundaries are set.

Access

Who can reach a memory or a digital identity, and with which permissions.

Review

How outputs are checked — and what the technology is not allowed to do.

Data and accountability

How we handle data, deletion and responsibility of use.
